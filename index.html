Suzanne H. Bijkerk, PhD

Lecturer, Finance Department · Bachelor Programme Manager, Economics and Business Economics
Erasmus School of Economics, Erasmus University Rotterdam

I am an economist working on information economics, the economics of markets and organizations, and the effects of discrimination on economic markets. Before returning to full-time academia I was an economic researcher at RaboResearch, and earlier I worked in auditing and finance at Deloitte and ABN AMRO. I hold a PhD in Economics from Erasmus School of Economics (Coping with Credit Risk, 2012, supervised by Prof. C.G. de Vries).
